THE WRONG CRATE
EPISODE 3
Vertical 9:16. About 60 seconds. One place. Dreamlike hand-painted 2D animation. Istanbul.
No establishing shot. The episode opens on: a motorbike key snatched out of a man's hand.

CHARACTERS
KEREM, 28. A delivery courier. Thick black handlebar moustache, dark grey flat cap, navy puffer jacket with one red chest stripe, dark green cargo trousers.
HASAN, 62. Owner of the tea house. Long grey moustache, charcoal flat cap, white shirt, brown knitted vest.
VERA, 34. Works for Mr. Kara. Pale blonde bob, wide black hat, long black coat with the collar up, black gloves, dark teal scarf.
MR. KARA, 55. Owns half the street. Heavy build, slicked grey hair, dark grey three-piece suit, gold ring.
AKSOY, 58. A regular at the corner table. White beard, round glasses, grey cardigan.
OLD MAN, 60. Plays backgammon with Aksoy. Bald, thick white eyebrows, green cardigan.

SCENE 1. OLD TEA HOUSE. JUST BEFORE DAWN.

Kerem snatches the motorbike key out of Mr. Kara's hand. The open crate of cash and the open black case stand on the counter. Aksoy and the old man watch from the corner table.

KEREM: One delivery. Then Hasan keeps his house.

Mr. Kara slams the lid of the crate shut.

MR. KARA: My crate. The harbor. Before sunrise.

KEREM: And the deed?

MR. KARA: When my money arrives. Not before.

Mr. Kara walks out. The door swings shut. Vera stays at the counter and lays her gloved hand on the crate.

VERA: Don't drive it. Keep it here.

KEREM: Why would you help us?

VERA: This crate can end him.

HASAN: Vera. He will know.

VERA: Not before sunrise.

At the corner table Aksoy opens his cardigan. A police badge shines on his belt. He looks at Kerem and nods once at the crate.

Hasan lifts the crate off the counter and sets it on the floor behind the counter. Hasan steps out from behind the counter.

The front door bangs open. Mr. Kara stands in the doorway.

MR. KARA: A thin door, Vera. I heard you.

Mr. Kara looks at the empty counter.

MR. KARA: Where is my crate?

KEREM: It was my idea. Not theirs.

Mr. Kara grabs Hasan by the arm and pushes him toward the door.

MR. KARA: Out of my tea house, old man.

HASAN: Forty years, Kara! Forty years!

Vera takes off her hat and steps between Mr. Kara and Hasan.

VERA: Take your hand off my father.
