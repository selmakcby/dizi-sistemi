THE WRONG CRATE
EPISODE 2
Vertical 9:16. About 60 seconds. One place. Dreamlike hand-painted 2D animation. Istanbul.
No establishing shot. The episode opens on: a stack of cash pulled out of an old man's vest pocket.

CHARACTERS
KEREM, 28. A delivery courier. Thick black handlebar moustache, dark grey flat cap, navy puffer jacket with one red chest stripe, dark green cargo trousers.
HASAN, 62. Owner of the tea house. Long grey moustache, charcoal flat cap, white shirt, brown knitted vest.
VERA, 34. Works for Mr. Kara. Pale blonde bob, wide black hat, long black coat with the collar up, black gloves, dark teal scarf.
MR. KARA, 55. Owns half the street. Heavy build, slicked grey hair, dark grey three-piece suit, gold ring.
AKSOY, 58. A regular at the corner table. White beard, round glasses, grey cardigan.
OLD MAN, 60. Plays backgammon with Aksoy. Bald, thick white eyebrows, green cardigan.

SCENE 1. OLD TEA HOUSE. NIGHT.

Kerem pulls the stack of cash out of Hasan's vest pocket and holds it up. The open crate of cash and the open black case stand on the counter. Aksoy and the old man watch from the corner table.

KEREM: You took it. She blamed me!

HASAN: One payment left. I was one stack short.

Hasan takes a folded paper from under the counter and lays it on the counter.

HASAN: The deed to this house. I owe Kara.

KEREM: You would pay Kara with Kara's money?

The front door opens. Mr. Kara walks in. Vera follows him and stops at the door.

MR. KARA: Every shop on this street pays me.

HASAN: I took it. Not the boy.

Kerem puts the stack of cash into the crate.

KEREM: Twenty-one. It's all here.

Mr. Kara picks up the deed from the counter, folds it and puts it inside his jacket.

HASAN: That paper is mine!

MR. KARA: It was. You stole from me.

Mr. Kara turns to Kerem.

MR. KARA: You gave it back. An honest courier.

MR. KARA: Vera. The key.

Vera walks to Mr. Kara and puts the motorbike key in his open hand. Mr. Kara holds the key out to Kerem.

MR. KARA: Drive for me tonight.

KEREM: And if I say no?

MR. KARA: Then Hasan sleeps on the street.

Mr. Kara holds the key over Kerem's closed fist. Aksoy and the old man look at Kerem.

MR. KARA: Choose now, Kerem.
