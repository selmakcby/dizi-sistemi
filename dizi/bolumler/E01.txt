THE WRONG CRATE
EPISODE 1
Vertical 9:16. About 60 seconds. One place. Dreamlike hand-painted 2D animation. Istanbul.
No establishing shot. The episode opens on: a wooden crate slammed onto a tea house counter.

CHARACTERS
KEREM, 28. A delivery courier. Thick black handlebar moustache, dark grey flat cap, navy puffer jacket with one red chest stripe, dark green cargo trousers.
HASAN, 62. Owner of the tea house. Long grey moustache, charcoal flat cap, white shirt, brown knitted vest.
VERA, 34. Works for Mr. Kara. Pale blonde bob, wide black hat, long black coat with the collar up, black gloves, dark teal scarf.
AKSOY, 58. A regular at the corner table. White beard, round glasses, grey cardigan.
OLD MAN, 60. Plays backgammon with Aksoy. Bald, thick white eyebrows, green cardigan.

SCENE 1. OLD TEA HOUSE. NIGHT.

Kerem slams a wooden crate with a hinged lid onto the counter and drops his motorbike key beside it. At the corner table Aksoy and the old man stop their backgammon game and look up.

KEREM: Delivery for Hasan.

Hasan stands behind the counter and lays both palms on the lid.

HASAN: This is not my tea, Kerem.

KEREM: Then whose is it?

Hasan lifts the lid. Stacks of cash fill the crate.

HASAN: I ordered tea. Only tea.

The front door bangs open. Kerem and the two old men turn to the door. Hasan's right hand slides off the crate and into his vest pocket. Vera walks in from the street with a black case in her right hand and stops at the counter beside Kerem.

VERA: You opened it.

Vera sets the black case on the counter and opens it. Tea tins fill the case.

VERA: Your tea, Hasan.

Vera runs a gloved finger across the stacks of cash.

VERA: Twenty. There were twenty-one.

KEREM: I never touched it!

VERA: You carried it. You pay.

Vera picks up the motorbike key from the counter.

KEREM: I'm a courier. I carry boxes!

VERA: Mr. Kara counts everything twice.

Vera walks to the front door and turns.

VERA: He is outside. He counts next.

VERA: Find it before he walks in.

Vera walks out with the key. The door swings shut.

Kerem turns to Hasan. Hasan's hand is pressed flat over his vest pocket. Kerem pulls the hand away. A thick stack of cash sticks out of the pocket.

KEREM: Hasan. What is in your pocket?
