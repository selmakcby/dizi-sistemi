THE WRONG CRATE
EPISODE 4
Vertical 9:16. About 60 seconds. One place. Dreamlike hand-painted 2D animation. Istanbul.
No establishing shot. The episode opens on: a woman shoved back against a tea house counter.

CHARACTERS
KEREM, 28. A delivery courier. Thick black handlebar moustache, dark grey flat cap, navy puffer jacket with one red chest stripe, dark green cargo trousers.
HASAN, 62. Owner of the tea house. Long grey moustache, charcoal flat cap, white shirt, brown knitted vest.
VERA, 34. Works for Mr. Kara. Pale blonde bob, wide black hat, long black coat with the collar up, black gloves, dark teal scarf.
MR. KARA, 55. Owns half the street. Heavy build, slicked grey hair, dark grey three-piece suit, gold ring.
AKSOY, 58. A regular at the corner table. White beard, round glasses, grey cardigan.
OLD MAN, 60. Plays backgammon with Aksoy. Bald, thick white eyebrows, green cardigan.
YOUNG COURIER, 20. A delivery boy. Red cap, yellow rain jacket. He never speaks.

SCENE 1. OLD TEA HOUSE. DAWN.

Mr. Kara lets go of Hasan and shoves Vera back against the counter. Hasan stands by the door. Aksoy and the old man watch from the corner table.

MR. KARA: Then you both sleep outside.

Kerem steps behind the counter, lifts the crate from the floor and slams it onto the counter.

KEREM: You asked where your crate was.

MR. KARA: Clever boy. Now carry it out.

KEREM: Mr. Kara. One question.

Kerem throws the lid open. Stacks of cash fill the crate.

KEREM: Whose money is this?

MR. KARA: Mine, you fool. Every stack.

Kerem points at the corner table.

KEREM: Louder. They didn't hear you.

MR. KARA: Mine! This whole street pays me!

Aksoy stands up.

AKSOY: Chief Inspector Aksoy. Three years of tea for this.

The old man stands and takes Mr. Kara by the arm.

AKSOY: Sergeant. Take Mr. Kara outside.

Vera reaches inside Mr. Kara's jacket and pulls out the deed. She puts it in Hasan's hands.

VERA: This was never yours.

VERA: Your house, father.

The old man walks Mr. Kara out the front door.

Sunlight comes through the front window onto the counter. Hasan slides a glass of tea across the counter to Kerem.

The front door bangs open. The young courier walks in and slams a small wooden crate onto the counter in front of Kerem.

Kerem lays both palms on the lid.
